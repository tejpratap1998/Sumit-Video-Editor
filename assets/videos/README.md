# Video Assets Directory

Place your `.mp4`, `.webm`, or `.mov` video files in this folder (`assets/videos/`).

### Recommended File Names for the 8 Showcase Cards:
- `video1.mp4` — Card 1: Lawn Care & Home Services Hook (Social Media)
- `video2.mp4` — Card 2: AJ Landscaping Client Testimonial (Social Media)
- `video3.mp4` — Card 3: Terra Craft Hardscaping Spotlight (Social Media)
- `video4.mp4` — Card 4: Evergreen Tree Care Urgent Call (Social Media)
- `video5.mp4` — Card 5: Kinetic Typography 3D Breakdown (Graphics & VFX)
- `video6.mp4` — Card 6: Multi-Slide Carousel Motion Reel (Graphics & Carousels)
- `video7.mp4` — Card 7: Apex Pro Commercial Launch Ad (Commercials)
- `video8.mp4` — Card 8: Lumina Studio Cinematic Teaser (Commercials)

### How It Works:
The video cards in `index.html` look for video files in `assets/videos/videoX.mp4`.
When a video file is placed here, the card automatically plays the video on hover / continuous loop with audio control, and plays inside the full-screen cinematic modal!
